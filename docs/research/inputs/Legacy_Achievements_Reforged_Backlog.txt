PURPOSE AND SCOPE
Target: approximately 200 new achievements. This draft contains exactly 200
backlog entries, including the original nine. They are candidates, not implemented
or approved additions. Existing custom achievements and native Legacy challenges
must be checked before finalizing the number of genuinely new additions.
All entries default to character scope. Backlog IDs are stable planning references,
not in-game IDs. Titles are working titles. Points and icons remain TBD.
Faction/class alternatives mean a character will see fewer than 200 eligible entries.
Shared class entries require separate criteria for each supported class.

DESIGN RULES
- Mix approachable milestones, long-term goals, exploration, and playful challenges.
- Favor Forever-specific additions once verified; do not invent server mechanics.
- Keep custom achievement points separate from native Legacy points.
- Validate tracking before implementation; historical progress may be unavailable.
- Preserve stable IDs when changing names or criteria.
- Retire or replace duplicates and untrackable entries instead of inflating the total.
- Use progression tiers where meaningful; avoid making every idea a counter grind.

TRACKING FEASIBILITY — PRELIMINARY, OCTOBER 11, 2026
Every candidate has a feasibility field. No entry is client-verified by this
review; no addon source or executable Forever client was available for testing.
These are implementation hypotheses and triage priorities, not confirmed support.

Three design paths:
1. STATE QUERY CANDIDATE: Ask the client for existing current/completed state.
   Examples: completed quest IDs, reputation standing, skill level, learned recipes.
   Exact APIs, returned scope, content coverage and availability require testing.
2. FORWARD COUNTER CANDIDATE: Count a reliable successful-action signal from
   tracking start onward. SavedVariables preserves observed progress between
   sessions, but cannot observe addon-disabled play or reconstruct missing history.
3. HOLD: Essential detection, attribution, provenance or historical evidence is
   unresolved. Redesign or replace if a reliable signal cannot be established.

Hearthstone finding:
Blizzard-generated API documentation mirrored in the live UI source defines
GetBindLocation with a string result. It does not expose a lifetime bind counter
in that function. ACH-017 now proposes distinct observed bind locations, rather
than promising a historical count or unique inn identity. HEARTHSTONE_BOUND is
only a candidate event to test; its presence and behavior are not verified here.
Repeat binds, localized names and multiple inns sharing a location need testing.

Other priority investigations:
- Campfires: Find a specific detectable aura, event, or state that distinguishes
  qualifying camping from ordinary sitting or rested state. Do not award from
  proximity to a presumed campfire or from an attempted sit/emote alone.
- Shared camping: Nearby players are not proof they share the same campfire.
- Fishing: Attribute loot to the successful fishing interaction, including stacks,
  junk, pools and interrupted casts. Ordinary loot chat is insufficient alone.
- Self-caught cooking: Identical stackable ingredients can be purchased, traded,
  banked or mixed. Provenance may be impractical; replace the condition if so.
- Transport: Being near a dock or on a moving platform is not journey completion.
- PvP: Team scores do not prove the player's personal flag/base action. Timed
  carrier/interrupt chains must identify the same opponent and qualifying outcome.
- Combat: A cast attempt or combat-log heal does not automatically prove a save,
  resurrection, successful summon, or recipient delivery.
- Merchant's Favor: Current balance is not lifetime earned or spent. Verify reward
  and purchase attribution independently from supply-faction reputation.
- Old beta/event feats: No historical proof means unknown, not automatic credit.
